Fleet Ops Console

Aircraft maintenance & fleet operations — live on fleet_ops_db

Fleet status

Reads vw_FleetStatus. Hours left use fn_HoursToNextService; amber means under 100 h, red means overdue.

Schedule maintenance

Calls sp_ScheduleMaintenance: grounds the aircraft, then reassigns or flags conflicting flights.

Maintenance events

Tasks

Add a task to this event

Use Complete on the event to run sp_CompleteMaintenance: finishes tasks, deducts stock, resets overhauled parts and restores airworthiness in one transaction.

Flights

Completing a flight adds hours and cycles to the airframe and its parts. If a critical part passes its limit, trg_FlagAOG puts the aircraft on AOG automatically.

Add a flight

Try adding a flight on a grounded or AOG aircraft: the database rejects it.
